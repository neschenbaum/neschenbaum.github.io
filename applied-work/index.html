Applied Work

Selected advisory, applied, and policy-research projects — competition cases, policy and regulation, energy markets, and mechanism design.

I work as an economic expert in competition proceedings and advise public bodies on algorithmic pricing, digital markets, energy, and AI regulation, mainly in Switzerland and the EU, in English and German. Engagements run through Swiss Economics; contact me at nicolas.eschenbaum [at] swiss-economics.ch.
