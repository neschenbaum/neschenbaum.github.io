I'm an economist and competition expert, and a Managing Economist and partner at [Swiss Economics](https://www.swiss-economics.ch/), where I lead the AI practice. I prepare expert reports in competition cases, advise authorities including the European Commission, and research algorithmic pricing, digital markets, and AI as an affiliated researcher at the [Zurich Center for Market Design](https://www.marketdesign.uzh.ch/en.html) (UZH).

## What I research

- **Algorithmic pricing and collusion:** designing and implementing pricing algorithms, and how rivals that share one algorithm provider compete.
- **Multi-agent AI risk:** how learning agents come to coordinate without instruction, and how to audit them from their policies alone.
- **AI markets:** market power, switching costs, and lock-in in foundation models ([research note](/applied-work/foundation-models-switching-costs/)).
- **Digital markets and regulation:** platform competition and the Digital Markets Act.
- **Energy markets:** pricing, market power, and the design of electricity-trading platforms.

See [Research](/publications/) for papers and [Applied Work](/applied-work/) for selected projects; my [CV](/cv/) has the full record.
